# HEALTH PASSPORT: Blockchain Meets AI for Wellness

## Overview
Health Passport is a decentralized digital healthcare platform designed to revolutionize the way medical data is managed and utilized. It combines blockchain's security and transparency with AI-driven health analytics, empowering users to control their health data and receive personalized wellness insights.

---

## Key Features

- **Blockchain Security:**
  - Ensures tamper-proof, immutable storage of medical records.
  - Provides transparent and traceable data sharing among healthcare actors.

- **Patient-Controlled Access:**
  - Users fully own and control access to their personal health data.
  - Permissioned sharing with doctors, labs, insurers, or caregivers.

- **Interoperability:**
  - Seamless integration across multiple healthcare providers and systems.

- **AI for Wellness:**
  - Predictive analytics for early risk detection and proactive interventions.
  - Personalized health and wellness recommendations based on aggregated data.

---

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** JavaScript, CSS
- **Smart Contracts:** Solidity
- **Backend/Data Analysis:** Python
- **Other Technologies:** Integrations for interoperability

---

## Example Use Cases

- Securely store and update your vaccination or medical history.
- Share prescription data with pharmacies with one click.
- Enable insurance companies to instantly verify claims.
- Receive AI-driven alerts on health risks and lifestyle guidance.

---

## Privacy & Security
- Full encryption of sensitive data.
- Tamper-resistant records via blockchain.
- Explicit consent for data sharing.
